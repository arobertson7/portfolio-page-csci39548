About Me

Hi there, I'm Austin! I'm completing my B.A in Computer Science at Hunter College this summer and beginning my career as a Software Engineer in August!

I enjoy software engineering because of the creativity involved in designing and building systems. It's fun picking the pieces to work with and putting them together in a uniquely efficient and reliable way. There are constant logic challenges to be solved, and there is always something new to be learned. The balance between applying creativity and logic to solve problems makes this field fun and rewarding.

Outside of work and school, I have many hobbies and am always on the lookout for new ones. I love to travel, learn foreign languages, play music, go to concerts, skateboard, play tennis, and play pool. It's a big, diverse world with many beautiful places to see, interesting people to meet, and fun acitivities to try. I enjoy exploring them!
